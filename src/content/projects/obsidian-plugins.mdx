## Overview

I developed and published six plugins on the Obsidian community directory. They cover PDF export, heading numbers, tables of contents, source line numbers, Mermaid diagrams, and multiple-choice questions.

## Plugins

- [Pagina](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/pagina) exports the open note to PDF on desktop, with page size, margins, headers, and footers.
- [Ordinal Headings](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/ordinal-headings) shows live Word-style numbering for Heading 1–6, with presets and custom patterns. The numbers stay visual unless you bake them into the note.
- [Conspectus](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/conspectus) inserts a live in-note table of contents from a `conspectus` block, including in a Pagina PDF.
- [Linearis](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/linearis) puts source-line numbers in a gutter on the left or right, in the editor and in reading view.
- [Mermaid Lumina](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/mermaid-lumina) opens Mermaid diagrams in a zoomable lightbox on a bright white canvas.
- [Quaestio](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/quaestio) turns `quaestio` blocks into multiple-choice questions in Reading view and saves the answers in the note.
