## Overview

During the Master in Computer Science at The University of Hong Kong (general stream, expected June 2028), I am developing an AI agent for market data.

The degree work focuses on data engineering and cybersecurity, and on how those apply to FinTech.

## What it integrates

- Asynchronous ETL pipelines
- A FastAPI persistence layer
- Market data ingested from YFinance
- Natural language as the way that data is requested
